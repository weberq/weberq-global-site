What we do

Three disciplines. One team.

A product needs electronics, mechanics and software that agree with each other. We design all three.

PCB design

Any board, from a small controller to a power stage.

  • Inverters and power converters
  • Battery management systems (BMS)
  • Embedded control and communication boards
  • Sensor, IoT and instrumentation boards
  • Custom boards for your use case

You receive schematic, PCB layout, BOM, Gerbers, drill and assembly files, and a 3D STEP model.

Enclosure and product design

The box around the board, and anything else that has to be made.

  • Enclosure boxes for electronics
  • Sheet metal, die-cast and moulded parts
  • Mounting, heat sinking and sealing
  • Cable entries and wiring layouts
  • Custom design solutions of any kind

You receive 3D CAD, 2D drawings, DXF flat patterns, BOM, exploded views and renders.

Software

The tools that people and machines actually use.

  • Financial dashboards and tools
  • Web portals and admin consoles
  • Android and iOS apps
  • APIs and backends
  • Desktop engineering software

You receive working software, source code, deployment and handover notes.

How we build

From the first trace to the closed box.

The same team designs the board and the enclosure, so connectors, heat and mounting holes agree before anything is made. Scroll to watch one come together.

  1. Placement

    Board outline, mounting holes and parts set around current paths, heat and connectors.

  2. Power first

    High-current paths stay short and wide, with clearance to suit the voltage on each net.

  3. Signals

    Gate drive, sensing and communication routed away from the switching noise.

  4. Copper and vias

    Ground pours, stitching vias and thermal vias under the parts that run hot.

  5. Fabrication files

    Each layer checked and released as the files your fabricator needs.

  6. Into the enclosure

    Bosses, gasket, cable entries and lid designed around the real board, not a guess.
